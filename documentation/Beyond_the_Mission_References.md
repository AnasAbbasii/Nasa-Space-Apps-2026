## 3. Planetary Cartography & Textural Sources

- **Moon Albedo & Topography:** Unified NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) Global Morphology Mosaic and USGS Astrogeology Science Center global albedo map (2048x1024 resolution UV-mapped equirectangular sphere).
- **Mars Surface Texture:** NASA Viking Orbiter Mars Global Color Mosaic, processed by the USGS Astrogeology Research Program, reflecting accurate albedo boundaries and true-color mineralogical hues.
- **Solar System Celestial Bodies:** High-resolution planetary texture maps provided by NASA's Solar System Exploration repository, including the Sun, Venus, Earth, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

## 4. Methodological Note on Coordinates and Verification

Landing site coordinates featured in the Beyond the Mission application reflect the sub-meter precision measurements established by:
1. **Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC):** Maged and confirmed all six Apollo Lunar Module descent stages (Apollo 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17) and Surveyor 3 at resolutions up to 0.5 meters per pixel.
2. **Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) HiRISE Camera:** Photographed the landed hardware, heat shields, parachutes, and rovers of Viking 1, Viking 2, Pathfinder/Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Phoenix, Curiosity, InSight, and Perseverance.
